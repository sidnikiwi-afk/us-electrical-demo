Zone, room and electrical layer

A room is a space closed in by walls. A zone is a part of the floor you choose to work on and print as one set of sheets. A zone can be a whole room, part of a room or several rooms. Waiting and till and Cutting floor share one room here, so there is no wall between them.

An electrical layer is a filter for kinds of work, such as lighting or sockets. Layers hide or show fittings across every zone. Zones decide which part of the floor and which walls you see.
