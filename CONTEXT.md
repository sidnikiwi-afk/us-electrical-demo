# Electrician Planner context

A job is a saved electrical planning record. A floor is the rectangular top-down drawing of a job. A wall is a physical boundary with a stable ID and viewable faces. A zone is a named rectangular work/print area; it does not create a wall. A layer filters fittings by work type across zones. Containment means trunking or conduit shown as editable sample runs; it is not priced or certified. Existing fitting labour totals and unknown-price warnings remain.
