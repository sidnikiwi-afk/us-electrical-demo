Quick guide

  1. A made-up job. A small fictional barber shop (6 × 4 m) getting new sockets, downlights and an LED strip.
  2. See the work. The floor plan and wall views are in section 2. The labour and material breakdown is in section 4.
  3. Compare versions. Use the Revision 1 / Revision 2 switch at the top to see the original plan against the client's changes.
  4. Try extras. In section 5, add sockets or downlights. Only the quote changes. The plan is not redrawn.
  5. Illustrative prices. Labour rates are invented and material prices are a snapshot. An electrician must check anything marked unknown before any work.
